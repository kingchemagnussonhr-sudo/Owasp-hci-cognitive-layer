# Cognitive Vulnerability Quick Assessment
## For Organizations Deploying AI Systems

This rapid assessment helps organisations estimate their exposure to
**cognitive vulnerabilities in AI-assisted decision systems.**

Score each statement:

0 = Not in place  
1 = Partially implemented  
2 = Fully implemented

---

## CV-01: Automation Bias

Automation bias occurs when users over-trust AI outputs and fail to independently verify them.

[ ] Users receive training on **when AI outputs should be questioned or verified**

[ ] High-risk AI outputs require **mandatory human review before operational decisions**

[ ] AI systems display **confidence intervals or uncertainty indicators**, not only point estimates

[ ] Monitoring systems detect **rapid acceptance patterns** (users approving AI outputs without review)

[ ] Incident reports classify **human-AI interaction failures**, including automation bias

---

## CV-02: Confirmation Bias

Confirmation bias occurs when users selectively accept AI outputs that support existing assumptions.

[ ] AI systems provide **alternative hypotheses or dissenting outputs by default**

[ ] Interfaces present **contradictory or contextual data** before confirmatory evidence

[ ] Decision workflows require **documented consideration of alternative interpretations**

[ ] Training programs include **AI-specific confirmation bias scenarios**

---

## Score Interpretation

0–4  
**High cognitive vulnerability exposure**  
Automation bias risks are likely unmanaged. Immediate governance controls recommended.

5–9  
**Moderate exposure**  
Basic safeguards exist but interface and workflow controls should be strengthened.

10–14  
**Low exposure**  
Core safeguards are present. Expand coverage to additional cognitive vulnerabilities
(e.g., authority bias, cognitive overload).
